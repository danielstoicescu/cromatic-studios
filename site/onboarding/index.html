01 · The Map

Tap a place.
We drive you there.

Cromatic City is a diorama of the studio's Bucharest. The streets are industries, the buildings are clients, and every place is numbered, labelled and waiting to be found.

  1. Step 1

    “Where do you want to start?”

    The map opens with a welcome card: tap a place, we drive you, see the work, say hi. Four doors in: Coffee Street, Fintech Boulevard, Medical Avenue, or straight to the studio.

  2. Step 2

    Labels on fine stems.

    Every place is numbered. Its label hangs on a hairline stem from the roof: quiet at rest, rich on hover, with a soft glow on the ground when it is open. Zoom in, zoom out, or tap ◎ to find the van.

  3. Step 3

    The van drives you there.

    Pick a place and the Mystery van takes the real roads of the map, the shortest way. Then the camera swings down, cinematic, to the building's front door.

  4. Step 4

    A card with the work.

    Every place card carries a picture, a few honest lines and one button to the full case. “Unpack the story.” “Lift the lid.” “Dig deeper.”

  5. Step 5

    Yards, shortcuts, side streets.

    Some clients are a whole neighbourhood. The Zdrovit yard holds a courtyard of health and beauty brands. The more you wander, the more you find: 50+ places, 43 of them waiting on the dock.

Cromatic Studios started in 2014 with two people and a company name written on a napkin. It was founded by Daniel Stoicescu, still the studio's creative architect. Today it is led by two partners who are also brothers: Daniel, the founder, and Cristian Stoicescu, the CEO. Ten people, one house, Strada Olari 9, Bucharest.

On the drive you pass every address the studio ever had, each with its plate: Muniției 5, Călinescu 54, Eliade 18, Dacia 99, and Olari 9, today.
