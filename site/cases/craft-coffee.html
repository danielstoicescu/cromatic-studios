Professional machines, sold by phone

Craft Coffee distributes and services professional espresso and brew equipment, chosen only from the most respected brands in the world. It is the official distributor of La Marzocco, Mazzer, PuQPress and Fellow in Romania, and also brings in Sinoova, Mahlkönig, Anfim and BWT.

It was born in 2023 out of a passion for La Marzocco machines, and is run by the team behind Steam Coffee Shop. It sold through phone calls and visits alone, with no shop of its own online.

Fellow brings the other half of the catalogue: kettles, grinders, scales and brewers for home and pour-over. Craft Coffee carries it alongside Mazzer grinders, made in Italy since 1948, and PuQPress, the first automatic coffee tamper in the world.
