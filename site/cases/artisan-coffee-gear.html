Gear for people who take coffee seriously

Artisan sells coffee equipment to passionate customers: people who weigh their doses, time their pours and read up on every grinder before they buy it.

A catalogue alone does not speak to them. Artisan wanted a tailor-made environment with a personal voice, one that helps customers join the specialty coffee community and feel understood.

The One Who Knocks

We built the direction on three keywords: only the best coffee gear, understood, coffee community. The archetype is part Explorer, part Ruler, and every choice that followed, from type to the webshop, comes back to it.

Branding& Design

Ailerons for the mark, Gruppo and Gotham for everything else, and an olive, ink and paper palette that lets the equipment be the hero. The A of Artisan folds into a repeating chevron, and every machine is drawn as a technical illustration.
