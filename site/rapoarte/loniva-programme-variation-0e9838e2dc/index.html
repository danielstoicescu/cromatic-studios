Community

The community
comes first.

Empowerment for community activities.

Loniva sells longevity, but people renew for the people. Every treatment room seats one, so the community lives in the rhythms around them: the same six faces in a class, the same Friday evening at Iva's.

Every week · 8 weeksThe Circlea circle of six

One reformer class holds six people, so six is the unit. The same circle, the same slot, eight weeks in a row. It opens with a scan, closes with a second one and an evening at Iva's. Friends sign up together; strangers leave as friends.

Every FridayLate at Iva'smembers' Friday

After the last Form class, Iva's stays open an extra hour for members and one guest each. No programme, just the bar.

Every quarterScan Dayopen morning

Members re-scan, compare progress and stay for a short talk by a guest: a doctor, a nutritionist, a sleep coach. Results are personal; the morning is shared.

Every monthBring a Frienda guest pass

Every membership includes one guest a month, who gets the First Visit. Members recruit members, which is the cheapest marketing a club has.

Twice a yearGuest Edition at Iva'sguest brands at the bar

A local brand takes over the menu or the terrace at Iva's for a week: a bakery, a skincare line, a running brand. NUDES in Barcelona built half its community this way.

Every monthIva's Letterthe thread between them

Iva writes to every member once a month: the circles that finished, the milestones reached, the next Scan Day. The app reminds you that your circle meets tomorrow, not that your subscription renews.

The programme

Move. Measure.
Charge & Recover.

Three groups, each tied to a room. Move is the weekly habit that brings people back. Measure proves it is working. Charge & Recover is what nobody else in the neighbourhood has.

Five open points.

Approve the namesIva's, Form, Cryo, Light, Air, Rest and Scan go to Biofit's signage schedule once the client signs them off.
One person or two in AirBiofit's design intent prefers a single-user chamber, while the quotation is for the two-person VH2 and the coordination plan says model TBC. Two seats doubles capacity and allows a shared session.
Protocols need an ownerScreening, session limits and supervision for Cryo and Air are outside Biofit's design scope. They have to be written with the supplier and a medical advisor before opening.
The bar menu feeds Biofit's bar consultancyBiofit proposes a health bar consultancy from November to January covering recipes, costing and training. This menu is the brand-side input to that work; recipes, portions and allergens remain theirs to finalise.
Prices need a local checkPilates prices are benchmarked against Bucharest studios. Treatment prices have no verified local benchmark yet and should be checked before any package is printed.
